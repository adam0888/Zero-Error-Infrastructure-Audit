Section 01 — The Problem

Most infrastructure doesn't fail loudly. It fails quietly, then all at once.

These are the three failure classes we see most often in fast-growing teams — not a sequence, three independent exposures that usually exist at the same time.

[RISK_01]

Single Point of Failure

One primary database node. One region. One bad deploy away from a full outage — with no automated failover to catch it.

[RISK_02]

Silent Data Drift

Replication lag and schema mismatches nobody catches — until a customer does, in production, publicly.

[RISK_03]

Bill Shock at Scale

Reactive infrastructure spend that jumps 3–5x the moment real traffic shows up, because nothing was built to scale gracefully.

Section 07 — FAQ

Before you apply

How is this different from hiring a fractional SRE?
A fractional SRE is ongoing headcount. This is a fixed, 10-week, 5-phase engagement that ends with a certified architecture and a runbook library your own team owns — not a retainer that continues indefinitely.
Do you work with our existing database stack, or migrate us?
We work with your existing stack by default. Migration is only proposed in Phase 02 if the audit shows your current stack genuinely cannot support the redundancy target — and it's your call either way.
What does the engagement cost?
Pricing is scoped after the Phase 01 audit, since it depends on your infrastructure's current complexity. Applicants get exact numbers before anything is signed.
What if we don't have a dedicated infra engineer?
You'll need at least one engineering stakeholder who can join working sessions during Phases 03 and 04. We do the implementation; they do the sign-off and knowledge transfer.
Why only three slots per quarter?
Each engagement is hands-on across all five phases with the same team. Three concurrent cohorts is the limit before that quality drops.
